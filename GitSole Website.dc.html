11 · About — Desktop
We don't sell everything. We select what is worth buying.
Gitsole is a Pakistani thrift footwear brand. We buy branded pre-owned shoes, inspect them by hand, and list only the pairs we would be happy to wear ourselves. Everything else gets left behind.
The problem
Thrift shopping in Pakistan is a gamble.
Bulk sellers upload hundreds of pairs with one blurry photo and no honest description. Prices jump because of a logo. Delivery charges appear at the last step. You find out what you actually bought when the parcel opens.
What we do instead
We do the hard part before you see the shoe.
Sourcing, inspecting, cleaning, grading, photographing and pricing against real retail value. By the time a pair reaches the site, the decision left for you is whether you like it.
What we stand for
Quality over quantity
Fewer pairs, each one selected for a reason.
Best value
Competitive prices without dropping standards.
Honest listings
Flaws shown, condition graded, nothing hidden.
Nationwide access
Free home delivery to every city in Pakistan.
Wide variety
Nike, Adidas, New Balance, Jordan and more.
Customer first
A repeat customer beats a quick sale.
Quality over quantity. Every pair should be worth your money.
